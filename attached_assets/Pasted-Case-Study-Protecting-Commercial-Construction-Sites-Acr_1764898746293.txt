Case Study: Protecting Commercial Construction Sites Across Australia
Industry Context
Commercial construction projects in Australia face dual challenges: property crime and compliance oversight. Industry reports estimate that theft and vandalism cost businesses over $100 million annually, with copper wiring, generators, and building materials among the most common losses.

At the same time, PPE compliance is under increasing scrutiny. A 2024 audit found that up to 30% of workers on commercial sites were non-compliant with PPE protocols during the workday. Insurers and regulators now expect verifiable records of PPE usage as part of risk management frameworks.

The Challenge
High-value theft: Heavy machinery, wiring, and fixtures are prime targets, especially during overnight downtime.

Operational disruption: Vandalism and unauthorised access cause costly delays and insurance claims.

Compliance gaps: Manual PPE checks miss transient violations and lack timestamped evidence.

Fragmented oversight: Large sites with multiple subcontractors struggle to maintain consistent monitoring.

LinkVision’s Solution
LinkVision deployed a tailored surveillance and compliance system across commercial projects:

AI-powered surveillance units with perimeter breach and vehicle movement detection.

Cloud-based monitoring for managers and insurers, enabling remote oversight.

Smart deterrence tools including alarms, lighting, and voice-down features.

PPE Analytics Add-on to automatically detect helmet, vest, and boot usage at entry points and active zones.

Automated reporting to provide daily compliance summaries and audit-ready evidence.

Results
70% reduction in theft and vandalism incidents within the first 90 days of deployment.

Improved PPE compliance visibility, with automated detection achieving >95% accuracy.

Insurance premiums lowered, supported by verifiable compliance records.

Project timelines protected, with fewer interruptions linked to crime or compliance breaches.

Stakeholder confidence strengthened, especially among insurers and investors.
